Programmes

Train with a plan, not a guess

01

Strength Lab

Barbell and dumbbell training on a progressive 6-week block. Squat, hinge, push and pull — coached for form first, then load.

  • All levels
  • 60 min
02

HIIT Burn

Interval circuits with rowers, bikes, kettlebells and battle ropes. High effort, scalable moves, and a heart-rate screen to keep you honest.

  • Fat loss
  • 30–45 min
03

Box Fit

Pad work, bag rounds and footwork drills. No sparring, no experience needed — just a serious sweat and a lot of stress relief.

  • Beginner friendly
  • 50 min
04

Flow & Mobility

Joint prep, active stretching and core control to undo desk posture, recover from heavy days and move without stiffness.

  • Recovery
  • 45–60 min

1-to-1 coaching

Personal Training

A programme built around your goal — first pull-up, post-natal return, marathon prep or simply feeling stronger. Includes a movement assessment and progress check-ins every 4 weeks.
