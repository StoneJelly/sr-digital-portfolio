Our studio

A calm corner of PJ, made for you.

Serai & Silk takes its name from lemongrass — the scent of our signature scalp massage — and the finish we aim for in every service. We keep the studio small on purpose: one client per stylist, no rushing, and honest advice on what will actually suit you.

We use salon-grade, cruelty-free products and every appointment starts with a proper consultation.
